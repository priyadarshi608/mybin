car comes to entry designated area called entry terminal
entry terminal - car scanner, interactive display board, printer, lift
car scanner scans the car
entry terminal display board - car details, availability, confirmation button,
confirm - ticket is printed by printer
lift will take the car and park in the alloted spot

	create entry in VehicleEntry table
}

Class IDB
displayCarDetailsAndAvailability(carDetails, numberOfSpotsAvailable) : void - shows confirm button if numberOfSpotsAvailable > 0 else shows no parking available message - event handler of confirm button is confirmParking method of EntryTerminal class.
displayTarrif(amountToBePaid) : void - shows Pay button - event handler of this pay button is processPayment method of ExitTerminal class.

====================================================================================

person comes to exit designated area called exit terminal
exit terminal - ticket scanner, display board, payment portal, lift,
ticket scanner scans the ticket
tarrif calculator calculates tarrif
exit terminal display board - displays the tarrif, pay button,
pay botton - forwards to payment portal for payment,
lift will bring the car
